Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

At Spendly, your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Expense data — categories, amounts, dates, and descriptions you log inside the app.
  • Usage data — basic technical information such as your browser type, device, and the pages you visit.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Spendly service.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Generate summaries, breakdowns, and reports from the expenses you log.
  • Communicate important updates about the service.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share your data with trusted service providers who help us operate Spendly (such as hosting and analytics providers), and only to the extent necessary to deliver the service.

4. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, and data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5. Your choices

You can update or delete your account information at any time from your account settings. If you wish to delete your account entirely, contact us and we will remove your data from our active systems.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features of Spendly may not work properly without them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Spendly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at hello@spendly.app.
